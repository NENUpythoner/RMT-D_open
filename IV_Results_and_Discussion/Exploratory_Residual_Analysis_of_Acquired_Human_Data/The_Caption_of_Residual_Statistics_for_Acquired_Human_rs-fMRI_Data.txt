Residual statistics for acquired human rs-fMRI data.

(a) Profiles extracted along the three-dimensional spatial diagonal from voxel 1,1,1 to H,W,Z from Fig.4(g)--(l).
The horizontal axis denotes the voxel index along the path, and the vertical axis denotes the residual temporal standard deviation.
Mean and standard deviation in the legend denote the mean and standard deviation of the profile values at the common valid path locations, respectively.

(b) Number of foreground voxels at each time point for which there was insufficient evidence to reject Gaussianity.
The horizontal axis denotes the time point, and the vertical axis denotes the number of voxels satisfying p_{KS}\geq 0.05.
Mean and standard deviation in the legend denote the temporal mean and standard deviation of the voxel-count trajectory, respectively.
