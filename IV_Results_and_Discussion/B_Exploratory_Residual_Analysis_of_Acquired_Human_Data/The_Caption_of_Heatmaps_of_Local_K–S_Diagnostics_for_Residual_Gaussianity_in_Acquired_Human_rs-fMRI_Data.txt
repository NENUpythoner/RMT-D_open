Heatmaps of local K–S diagnostics for residual Gaussianity in acquired human rs-fMRI data.
The two rows correspond to slices 16 and 23, respectively.
The residuals were standardized using the RMT-D noise-scale estimate as a common normalization reference.
Each voxel displays -{log}_{10}\left[{mean}_{t}\{p_{KS}\left(v,t\right)\}\right], and the reference threshold is -{log}_{10}\left(0.05\right).
Values above this threshold correspond to a temporally averaged p value below 0.05, whereas the remaining values indicate insufficient evidence to reject Gaussianity under this descriptive summary.
The displayed values are not additionally truncated.
Gray regions in the VSTSVS results denote boundary locations that were not processed because of the preset kernel size and therefore contain NaN values.
